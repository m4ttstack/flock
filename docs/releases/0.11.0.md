## New

- Saved herdr machines (`herdr machine add`) show up as profiles. Switching to one runs Flock against that machine's herdr over ssh, with live panes. Pick a profile at the top of the sidebar (⌃⌘P), or hold ⌃` to flip profiles and ⌥` to flip herdr servers.
- A calmer look: neutral chrome in place of accent blue, one title bar row holding the view switcher, tabs and notices, and pill tabs.
- Settings > Panes chooses how panes sit (edge to edge or boxed), how the focused pane stands out (drain colour, dim others, quiet chrome and more, with dimness and desaturation sliders), and a status line under each pane's header (aurora, comet, glint, breathe or off).
- Claude Code's Auto theme follows Flock's light or dark theme. It needs the updated herdr mouse patch, which Settings and a banner offer to anyone on the earlier one.
- Shell panes get a compact header that grows when an agent starts, and untitled agent panes show their repo and branch.

## Changes

- Chat and rt menus open as flat panels hanging from their button.
- Quick launch shows only on the focused pane.
- Notifications drop the jump arrow and highlight on hover.
- Light themes give terminals colours that read on a light background.

## Fixes

- A new tab always gets its tab button. Opening one right after closing another could leave it without one, and closing it then showed "No tab selected".
- Closing a tab focuses a pane in the tab it lands on.
- Shrinking a pane no longer draws its terminal under the header while the resize is held.
- A divider's grab area no longer reaches into the terminal beside it.
