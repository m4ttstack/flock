## Changed

- The ⌃Tab workspace switcher leaves out herd workspaces, as the rail does.
- ⌃⌘← and ⌃⌘→ wrap around at the ends of the tab strip.
- A pane you named keeps its name, even when its program sets a title of its own.
- Moving a tab's only pane to a new tab or workspace takes the tab's name with it.

## Fixes

- When a program exits and its tab closes, the tab and its notifications leave flock straight away. Before, the notification stayed and clicking it opened the wrong tab.
- Status dots on tabs and workspaces always match their panes, including after a tab closes.
- Opening a pane no longer freezes flock for seconds at a time when the disk is busy.
