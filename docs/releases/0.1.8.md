## What's new

**Choose where right-clicks go.** A pane whose program uses the mouse shows a small mouse in its title row. Right-clicks go to the program by default, and ⌥-right-click opens flock's menu. Click the mouse, or press ⌥⌘M, to swap the two for that pane; its right button lights up while clicks go to the program. Each pane remembers its choice.

**Start herdr from flock.** If herdr isn't running yet, say after a restart, flock says so and offers a Start herdr button instead of an empty window.

**A grip to move panes.** Drag a pane by the dots at the top middle of its title row. The rest of the title bar no longer starts a drag; ⌘R still lets you drag from anywhere.

## Changed

- The title row's buttons are bigger, and everything in it has a hover state, sidebar rows too.
- The chat button shows only on panes running Claude Code.
- The rt modal's close button is bigger, with more room before the size buttons.
- The launcher's caption is gone when your agents were found.
