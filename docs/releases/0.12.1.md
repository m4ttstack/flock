## Changes

- Programs in a pane can show kitty graphics images, including ones drawn with Unicode placeholders. This needs the updated herdr patch, which Flock offers to install.
- Panes have a close button beside zoom. It closes the pane the same way ⌘W does, and its warning hangs from the button the way a tab's does.
- A close warning raised by right-clicking a pane pops out of the pointer and grows toward the pane's middle. Warnings over the canvas or hung from a tab arrive and leave the same quick way.
- A right-click close picked outside the window opens its warning at the nearest window edge instead of the middle.
- The close warning's agent line is shorter: "Stops 1 running agent", or "Stops 3 running agents in 2 panes".
- The name of what is closing sits under the title with an icon, so it no longer runs into the pane's own title.
