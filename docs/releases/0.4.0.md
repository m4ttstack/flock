## New

- Give a pinned workspace its own Claude account. Right-click the pin and pick one under Claude Account, and Claude launched there from Flock runs as that cswap account. The menu appears only when cswap is installed. If the account is gone or cswap can't be read, Claude launches on the current login and Flock tells you why.
- Overview cards and Arrange tiles show the chat handle of a pane that is signed in to chat.

## Changed

- ⌘W closes the top-bar overlay, or any open modal, before it closes a pane.

## Fixes

- Input methods, dictation and the emoji picker open at the cursor instead of at the pane's corner.
