## New

- Settings > Starting Folder sets where a new workspace, tab or pane starts: the current pane's folder, the repo's main checkout, your home folder, or a folder you choose. New tabs start in the repo's main checkout by default, or in your home folder outside a repo.
- A tab you haven't named that holds one pane shows that pane's title, behind a small pane mark. Renaming the tab gives it your name instead.
- The ⌥Tab tab switcher shows who in each tab is signed in to chat, as "ivy" or "ivy · 2 more". It only appears where chat is set up.

## Changed

- The command palette shows ⌘1, ⌘2 and ⌘3 beside rt cd, Claude and Codex.

## Fixes

- ⌘1, ⌘2 and ⌘3 work in any pane whose shell is at its prompt, not only in a brand new pane.
- The ⌥Tab tab switcher names an unnamed tab after its pane even after other tabs have been closed or moved.
