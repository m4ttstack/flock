## New

- Flock runs on Intel Macs. The app is universal, and Settings offers the herdr 0.9.5 mouse patch built for Intel.
- Chat can open a Slack support chat from a pane. It shows in a sidebar on that pane's tab and runs on a background agent, which exits when the chat closes.
- A new pane top bar carries the chat handle's name and face, with an unread dot. Faces come from `rt chat avatar` when the installed rt has it, and fall back to an initial.
- Terminal panes show quick launch as a slim keycap strip on the pane's bottom edge. It stays out of the way of typing and steps aside when the screen reaches it.

## Changes

- The band above a pane's terminal is a little taller, so a pane can show one fewer terminal row.
- Tooltips hang below their control in the theme's inverted colours.

## Fixes

- Dragging a tab to the end of the open tabs no longer sends it to the front when the workspace has complete tabs, and the drag preview no longer opens a gap beside the moved tab.
