## Changes

- A pane that another client takes over, such as an editor's terminal attaching to it, now says "Open in another client" instead of showing the loading sheep forever. Click Take it back, or press Return in the pane, to bring it back to Flock.
- Flock no longer takes such a pane back on its own when its window comes back on screen. It stays with the other client until you take it back.
- A pane herdr refuses to reattach says so too, with a Reconnect button.
