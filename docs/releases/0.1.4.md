## What's new

**The loader trots.** A pane that takes a moment to attach now shows the ram hopping slowly along the bottom edge, leaving a winding trail of dots behind it. Panes that attach quickly still show nothing.

**⌘J opens the oldest notification.** It jumps to the bottom card in the dock and clears it, same as clicking it. Press it again to work up the stack. It lives in the View menu next to Clear Notifications (⌘K).

**rt cd in the launcher.** When `rt` is on your PATH, a new pane offers an rt cd button ahead of the harnesses. After the picker, the launcher comes back so you can still start claude or codex.

## Fixes

- Panes attach faster. Flock's per-pane pipes and configs no longer live in the shared temp folder, where a crowded folder could stall an attach for seconds.
- A loading pane no longer flashes a "click to focus in herdr" card.
- The launcher buttons no longer appear, vanish and come back while a new tab loads.
- The Copied pill is roomier, stays up for 2 seconds, and now also shows for copies made in apps that take over the mouse.
