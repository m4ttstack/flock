## New

- Mark a tab complete from its right-click menu. A complete tab shows a checkmark, dims its title and moves into a compact stack at the far right of the strip; hovering one lifts it out of the stack. Unmarking it puts it back where herdr has it.
- In All Workspaces, a click on a pane opens a preview of its latest output, and a double-click goes to that pane. The preview has an Open pane button and a close button, and Esc or a click on empty space puts it away.

## Changed

- All Workspaces shows every tab of every workspace and fits as many per row as the window allows, instead of folding extra tabs behind a "+N" tile.
- The pane preview is about twice as wide and shows up to 20 lines. Long lines wrap instead of being cut off, and the output scrolls, following new output while you stay at the bottom.
- Tabs grow to 240pt before their title is cut off.
- The right end of the tab strip shows the version of herdr you're connected to, such as "herdr v0.9.3", in place of a protocol number that never changed.

## Fixes

- In All Workspaces, a drop aimed at the first tab of a card's second or later row lands on that tab.
- The All Workspaces button's icon is centered in its hover highlight.
