## Changes

- Close warnings are now a card in the window instead of a sheet, so they appear and go away at once. A close from a tab's close button or menu hangs the card from that tab, a right-click close puts it at the pointer, and a keyboard close shows it over the tab. Tab and the arrow keys move between its buttons, Return or Space picks one, and Esc cancels.
- The close warning names what is closing and says how many agents it would stop.
- Closing a tab or a pane takes effect at once, without waiting on herdr to finish stopping what was running in it.
- The workspace group close, the herdr patch confirm and the top bar refusal use the same card.
- The Boxed pane layout boxes a tab only when it shows more than one pane. A lone or zoomed pane fills the canvas, and Settings says so.
- Boxed panes get a little more room at the window's edges, and their outer corners follow the window's.
- The Dimness setting can go all the way to none.
- ⌘\` switches between agents, named and pictured as chat knows them.

## Fixes

- A pane whose connection to herdr ended, most often while a freshly started server restored its panes, no longer stays blank: it reconnects on its own.
- The chat sidebar's header sits in the title bar's row, and the title bar's controls no longer draw over its End chat button.
- The Slack support chat reads its persona from rt's new setting.
