## What's new

**Command palette.** Press ⌘K to search everything flock can do: pane commands, rt, chat, view and more, each with its shortcut. Recently used commands sit at the top. Esc or ⌘K again closes it.

**Workspace switcher.** Hold ⌃ and press Tab to see your workspaces, most recently used first. Keep pressing Tab (⇧Tab goes back) and let go of ⌃ to switch. A quick ⌃Tab jumps straight back to the last workspace. The order is kept across restarts.

**Keys for tabs.** ⌃⌘← and ⌃⌘→ move to the previous and next tab.

## Changed

- Clear Notifications is now ⇧⌘K, since ⌘K opens the palette.
- Move Pane is now ⌃⌥⌘ plus an arrow, freeing ⌃⌘ arrows for tabs.
- ⌘+ and ⌘- step through the text sizes; ⌘0 is gone.
- The rt window keeps its own text size and box size for each rt command, separate from the panes.
- The rt menu calls glitter "Review and commit".

## Fixes

- ⌘+ and ⌘- change flock's text size only, stepping from the size on screen.
