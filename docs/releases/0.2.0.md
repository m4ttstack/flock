## New

- All Workspaces is now two views, Overview and Arrange, beside Workspaces. Switch between the three with ⌘1, ⌘2 and ⌘3.
- Overview sorts every pane into three lanes: Needs you (blocked, then done), Working (working, then busy in the background) and At rest. Each lane shares its height between its two groups, and scrolls them separately only when both are long.
- Opening a card in Overview shows that pane on its own, with the workspace and pane name, a Next button for the next pane that needs you, and a "Queue clear" badge once nothing does. Going back to Workspaces returns to the pane you left it on.
- Arrange shows each pane's live screen, in its own colours, inside thumbnails that fill the window. Zoom into one workspace from its header, a double-click or Space, and press Esc to zoom out.
- In Arrange, a click selects a pane and a double-click or Return opens it. Right-click a pane for Copy Output, Rename Pane and Close Pane. Typing never reaches a terminal from Arrange.
- Pin a workspace from its menu, or drag it into the new PINNED section of the sidebar. A pinned workspace keeps its place after its last tab closes, and clicking it opens it again in its home folder.
- Each workspace has its own symbol, which you can change from its menu. Colour in the sidebar now only ever means status.
- A Claude pane that is idle but still running a background shell or monitor shows a mauve ring with the reason, such as "1 shell · 3m", instead of looking finished.

## Changed

- A blocked pane stays in Needs you until it is unblocked.
- A new pane's launcher keeps ⌘1, ⌘2 and ⌘3 while it is open.
- Corners, selection, hover and header heights are consistent across the app.
- Workspaces with no tabs are dimmed, and an empty Workspaces heading is hidden.
- The view shortcut hint no longer sits in the top right corner.

## Fixes

- Opening a done pane from Overview clears its done state, as visiting it from Workspaces does.
- The window title no longer crowds the tab strip.
- Counts in the sidebar line up.
- The Herds icon matches the other workspace symbols in size and alignment.
