## New

- Chat ships inside Flock. It no longer needs the herdr chat plugin installed, only `rt`.
- ⌥Tab switches between tabs the way ⌃Tab switches between workspaces. A tab with no name shows its focused pane's title.
- ⇧⌘Return zooms the focused pane, and again unzooms it. A zoomed tab shows an Unzoom button in the pane legend.
- Chat shows people's display names, not just their handles, in the pane legend, the chat popover, peek, broadcast and quick send.

## Changed

- The View menu is split by scope. A new Pane menu holds split, zoom, focus, move, swap and launch. File has Close Pane (⌘W), Close Tab (⇧⌘W) and Close Workspace (⌥⇧⌘W).
- Every pane direction shortcut sits on ⌥⌘ (add ⇧ to move, ⌃ to swap). ⌃⌘ arrows step through tabs and workspaces, ⌃1-9 goes to a tab and ⌃⌘1-9 to a workspace.
- The pane legend's status chip leads the title, and idle panes show one too.
- Settings opens in its own window, taller and resizable in height, and ⌘W closes it. The rt modal text sizes moved there from the View menu.

## Fixes

- A pane that starts an agent after it opens now gets its chat button right away, not on the next refresh.
