## Changes

- An image pasted into an agent's prompt now shows as a chip in place of its `[Image #N]` label, with a tray of the prompt's images above it. Hover over one for a larger preview.
- Drop files and images on a pane. A file dropped on Claude Code's prompt arrives as one short label with a chip, instead of its whole path.
- Click a chip or a tray image to open it in a preview panel, which grows from what you clicked. Arrow keys step through the images, animated GIFs play, and Escape, Space or a click elsewhere closes it.
- A conversation's images are still there after Flock restarts, for 14 days after they were last on screen.

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/m4ttstack/flock/compare/v0.13.1...v0.14.0
