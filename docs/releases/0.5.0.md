## New

- Drop a pane or tab on an empty pinned workspace to open it there, in a workspace named for the pin.
- The Move To menu lists tabs and workspaces under their own headers, shows each workspace's symbol, and includes empty pinned workspaces. Board's review workspaces and herds are left out.

## Changed

- The Workspaces tab has its own icon, a sidebar beside a pane, instead of a two-column version of Overview's.
- Closing a pane moves focus to the pane beside it.
